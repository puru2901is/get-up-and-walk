What your watch does today

Sitting alerts and hourly goals exist. You just can't see them where you look.

Galaxy Watch, built in

An inactivity alert after 50 minutes of sitting. The timer is fixed, and there's no step goal to hit.

Pixel Watch, built in

With Get Up and Walk Reminder

This hour's count is a ring on your watch face. You pick the goal and the hours, and the buzz at :50 comes only when you're short.

One hour, start to finish

How an hour works

A desk morning, 10:00 to 11:00. A few short walks get you to 182 steps by 10:50, so the watch buzzes. A walk to the kitchen finishes the hour.

  1. 10:00

    The count starts at zero

    Every hour is a fresh goal, so a lazy morning doesn't follow you all day.

  2. 10:00–10:50

    Walk whenever it suits you

    The ring fills as you move. Glance at the time and you see it.

  3. 10:50

    Short? The watch buzzes

    It shows the steps left. Already at 250? It stays quiet.

  4. Goal reached

    The heart lights up

    The buzz stops and the hour counts toward today's total, like ❤ 4/5.

Questions

Which watches does it work on?

Wear OS 3 or newer with a step sensor: Galaxy Watch4 and every Galaxy Watch since, every Pixel Watch, and other Wear OS watches. It doesn't run on Apple Watch, Fitbit Versa, Sense, Charge or Inspire, or Garmin.

What does a beta tester do?

When testing opens, you get an email with a link to install the test build from Google Play. Use the same Google account you signed up with, keep the app installed for at least two weeks, and tell us when a reminder comes when it shouldn't, or doesn't come when it should.

What does lifetime access mean?

You pay once, or nothing as a beta tester, and get every future version of the app with no subscription.

Does it send my steps anywhere?

No. The app counts and stores your steps on the watch. It has no account and no server, and it doesn't use the internet.

Do I need to change my watch face?

No. Add the hourly steps complication to any watch face slot that accepts one: long-press the watch face, tap Customize, pick a slot, then choose Hourly Steps.
